PROFESSIONAL AGENTIC ARCHITECT

Apex BioHealth Case Study

Company Overview

Apex BioHealth is a global biopharmaceutical research organization and clinical trial sponsor developing targeted oncology and rare-disease therapeutics. Apex coordinates Phase I–III clinical trials across 350 hospital sites worldwide, generating petabytes of multimodal clinical data including patient electronic health records (EHRs), radiology imaging, pathology audio dictations, and genomic sequences.

Solution Concept

Apex BioHealth is architecting an autonomous Clinical Trial Intelligence & Regulatory Submission platform on Google Cloud. Using Gemini Enterprise and custom multimodal ADK agents, clinical researchers will query unstructured trial protocols, patient adverse event logs, and medical imaging to accelerate FDA/EMA regulatory dossier preparation.

Given strict HIPAA and GxP compliance mandates, every agentic retrieval and synthesis step must enforce cryptographic data access controls, automated PHI de-identification, verifiable source citations, and continuous clinical accuracy evaluation.

Existing Technical Environment

Clinical trial documents (PDFs, DICOM images, WAV audio dictations) are stored in Cloud Storage buckets protected by Customer-Managed Encryption Keys (CMEK). Structured clinical trial metadata resides in BigQuery and Cloud SQL.

Researchers currently spend months manually cross-referencing adverse event reports against historical literature. Early RAG prototypes suffered from low retrieval recall on medical acronyms, inability to parse embedded tables/charts in clinical PDFs, and lack of verifiable citation grounding.

Business Requirements

   ● Reduce clinical trial regulatory dossier preparation cycles from 6 months to 3 weeks.
   ● Achieve 99.5% citation accuracy with zero ungrounded medical claims in synthesized clinical summaries.
   ● Guarantee strict HIPAA, GDPR, and FDA 21 CFR Part 11 compliance with immutable audit trails.
   ● Enable natural-language cross-modal querying across clinical text protocols, radiology imagery, and physician audio notes.
   ● Provide clinical safety officers with transparent explainability into how agents correlate adverse event signals.

Technical Requirements

   ● Ingest and chunk complex multimodal clinical documents (text, tables, medical imagery, audio transcripts) using RAG Engine and Agent Retrieval with domain-specific embedding models.
   ● Configure Sensitive Data Protection (DLP) inspection and de-identification templates in the ingestion and agent gateway pipelines to redact PHI before external model inference or logging.
   ● Orchestrate sequential and parallel clinical review workflows in ADK where a Synthesis Agent generates regulatory drafts and a dedicated Verification Agent cross-checks every claim against retrieved source chunks.
   ● Enforce strict Agent Identity and Principal Access Boundary (PAB) policies so researchers can only retrieve clinical trial data for studies matching their IRB authorization scope.
   ● Implement continuous evaluation pipelines using the Agent Platform Gen AI Evaluation Service and custom medical LLM-as-a-judge autoraters to measure groundedness, completeness, and factual precision against golden clinical QA sets.
   ● Deploy production agent workloads on Agent Runtime within a VPC Service Controls (VPC-SC) perimeter.

Executive Statement

In clinical research, a single hallucinated dosage or misattributed adverse event can jeopardize patient safety and regulatory approval. Our agentic architecture on Google Cloud pairs multimodal Gemini intelligence with ruthless verification, deterministic PHI protection, and continuous golden-dataset evaluation.

Dr. Aris Thorne, Chief Medical Informatics Officer, Apex BioHealth
